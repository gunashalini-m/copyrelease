Invoice numbering

Current (GST) and Savings (Non-GST) invoices have separate prefixes and sequence counters. Choosing Savings on a new invoice switches to the Non-GST prefix and next number automatically. Saving an invoice also advances only that series.

Google Drive

Connect the Gmail account that owns your Drive, then choose a folder. Invoices are uploaded as PDFs. Create a Google Cloud OAuth Client ID (Web application), enable the Drive API, and add this page’s origin (for example http://127.0.0.1:4173) under Authorized JavaScript origins. Open the app in Chrome or Edge over http:// — Google sign-in does not work from a double-clicked file:// page.
